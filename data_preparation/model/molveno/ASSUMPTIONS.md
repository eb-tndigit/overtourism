# Molveno: register of decisions and assumptions

Every modelling decision (`D*`) and assumption (`A*`) of the Molveno data-preparation pipeline
and of the Molveno model, with its evidence and the places where it is used. The code refers to
these IDs in comments; a value without an ID is a bug.

Status: **in use** · **to review** (in use, to be reviewed) · **to validate** · **planned** ·
**retired**.

Paths: `pipeline/` = `data_preparation/model/molveno/`, `model/` = `executor-backend/src/model/molveno/`.
Validation figures: `python -m model.molveno.estimator_comparison` (leave-one-year-out on the
training summers: train on two, test on the third; R², CRPS of the predictive distribution,
coverage of the 90 % interval).

## Assumptions

| ID | Assumption | Evidence / reason | Used in | Status |
|----|------------|-------------------|---------|--------|
| A1 | Weather classes: rainy = rain ≥ 5 mm; cool = not rainy and tmax below the month's climatological mean − 2 °C; good = the rest. Climatology: station T0414, 2023–2025. | Same definitions as Fazzon (A19 there), not re-tuned for Molveno. Shares in the summers 2023–2025: good 0.61, rainy 0.20, cool 0.18. | `pipeline/presence_estimators.py` (`RAINY_MM`, `COOL_DEGREES`, `weather_climatology`) | in use |
| A2 | Public holidays of the summer: 2 June, 15 August. School holidays are captured by the day of the summer (their dates move little from year to year). | — | `pipeline/presence_estimators.py` (`HOLIDAYS`) | in use |
| A3 | The weather of Molveno is measured at Meteotrentino station T0414 San Lorenzo in Banale (Pergoletti), 685 m, 9 km from Molveno: the reference station of the area "Giudicarie esteriori e Molveno" in the open-data service. | Station T0383 Molveno (835 m) is not available in the open-data service used (`datiStoriciCaratteristiciSerie*UnArea`). | `data_preparation/data_download/download_meteo_meteotrentino.py` (T0414), `molveno.yaml` | to review |
| A4 | A weather class of a scenario day stands for the mix of weather days of that class observed in the same month of the training summers. | — | `pipeline/presence_estimators.py` (`gbm_presence_stats`) | in use |
| A5 | Frequency of the weather classes per month: the shares of the training summers. | 3 summers, ~30 days per month and year. | `pipeline/presence_estimators.py` (`weather_frequencies`); `model/molveno_presence_stats.py` (`weather_weights`: weighted by the days of each month in the period) | in use |
| A6 | Gradient boosting hyperparameters fixed (300 iterations, learning rate 0.05, 15 leaves, ≥ 10 days per leaf, L2 1.0), not tuned. | Tuning on 366 days with 3 folds would overfit the validation. | `pipeline/presence_estimators.py` (`GBM_PARAMS`) | in use |
| A7 | Parking capacity: the car spaces of the public car parks of the comune, 713 (disabled spaces included), uniform 683–743: 529 paid (Ischia 195, Lungolago 80, Seggiovia 61, Centro storico 60, Palazzetto 50, Piscina 30, Rio Massò 28, Pozze 25) and 184 with parking disc or free. Hotel and private spaces are outside the constraint. | `Parcheggi_Molveno_aggiornato-20240919.xlsx` (Comune di Molveno, September 2024; data lake `molveno/`). On the busiest days of 2023 (13–20/8, 9 and 16/7) the paying vehicles are 1,040–1,170 with 5.1 h stays: the 529 paid spaces × ~2, full. | pipeline `standardize.py` (`parking_lots`); `model/molveno_model.py` (`i_c_parking`), `molveno_backend.py` | in use |
| A8 | Excursionists arriving by car and looking for a public car park: uniform 0.70–0.86 (2.5 persons per car, A9). | Survey of summer 2023: 78 % of the excursionists by car. PAT counter M47: cars entering the beach area ≈ 830 + 0.31 per excursionist (R² 0.87), 0.31 × 2.5 = 0.78. Cars entering Molveno from Andalo and S. Lorenzo: 3,000–4,650 per day on average by month (4,600–5,600 on the busiest days), through traffic and tourists included. The paying vehicles do not measure it: on July weekdays 96 per day for ~3,000 excursionists and cars, against a paid supply of ~1,050 cars per day (unreliable, to ask the comune). | `model/molveno_model.py` (`i_u_excursionists_parking`) | to validate |
| A9 | Paid parking turnover of excursionists: 2 cars per space and day. Persons per car 2.5, tourists using the paid car parks 0.02 and their turnover 1.05: not re-estimated. | Mean paid stay 5.1 h on the busy days of 2023 (stays up to 24 h), over the paid day 8–18. Paying vehicles do not depend on the tourists (least squares coefficient 0). | `model/molveno_model.py` | in use |
| A10 | Beach: usage (people going to the beach in the day) 0.35 of the tourists and 0.78 of the excursionists; turnover 2.1 for both (tourists uniform 1.6–2.4); capacity uniform 4,700–5,500 (no source: from the 2023 maximum, not a saturation, so that the constraint shows in the field without being critical on the observed days). | BS4 counters at the 9 gates, 55 days of summer 2023. The gates count people in both directions (BS4): entries per day = passages / 2 ≈ 0.35 × tourists + 0.78 × excursionists (no intercept, R² 0.81; resampled days 10–90 %: 0.25–0.47 and 0.65–0.87; with an intercept the tourists' share goes to 0: tourists and excursionists move together, r 0.82). Turnover = entries / maximum present (the maximum cumulated difference of entries and exits): 2.1, half the days 1.6–2.4, not separable between tourists and excursionists. Maximum present 4,676: not a saturation. | `model/molveno_model.py`, `molveno_backend.py` | to review |
| A11 | Accommodation: the ISPAT perimeter, hotel and extra-hotel beds (the campsite is an extra-hotel establishment: Camping Spiaggia, ~800 places, would be most of the 1,017–1,148 extra-hotel beds; no breakdown by type in the ISPAT tables, to confirm), uniform 3,429 (2023) – 3,503 (2025; 3,477 in 2024); usage uniform 0.65–0.90 of the Vodafone tourists, allocation factor 1.06 (people per declared bed when the beds are full). Private rentals (1,278–1,314 beds) and second homes (1,219) are outside. | ISPAT daily overnight stays / Vodafone tourists, summer 2023: median 0.80, 10–90 % of the days 0.64–0.93 (r 0.93; August 0.63–0.86, lower as the beds fill up); August maximum 3,619 on 3,429 beds (2023) and 3,589 on 3,365 (2022): 1.06; the stays measure the occupancy, capped by the beds, the model the demand (tourists × usage): usage above capacity means beds full, the excess demand going elsewhere (private rentals and second homes, which Vodafone counts, or nearby villages, whose guests may come back as day visitors), not modelled. Days at saturation (stays within 4 % of the summer maximum): 15 (2022) and 11 (2023) of the 31 August days, none in June, July and September; model, August: 64 % sustainable, about 11 days. Backtest of the usage on the 2023 days, expected days of demand above capacity: July 3.2 (observed 0), August 13.3; a usage factor by day type or a narrower range does not remove the July excess without losing August: with the same tourists, the beds hold a smaller share in July than in August. The share falls as the tourists grow (0.88 below 2,000, 0.67 above 5,000): the signature of the saturation. | `model/molveno_model.py` | in use |
